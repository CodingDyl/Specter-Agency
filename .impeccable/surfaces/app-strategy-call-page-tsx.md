# Ready-to-move website strategy call

- Scope: `/strategy-call`; visitor mode: Persuade. This surface is a separate high-intent path, not a duplicate Growth Audit.
- Audience/job: a law-firm owner or decision-maker who has already decided that a new website, strategic redesign or related visibility system must move and needs a credible first decision.
- Primary action: send a commercially useful brief, then choose a time when the external calendar is configured. Secondary action: move exploratory visitors back to the Growth Audit.
- Proof/content: owner-centred call outcomes, a concrete four-part agenda, readiness signals, preparation guidance, and a qualification brief covering project need, timing, decision role and investment readiness.
- Direction: extend Black Label with a blue-hour decision scene, warm-paper deliberation, charcoal agenda and a focused carbon brief.
- Memorable moment: “When the website cannot wait.” makes the route’s urgency unmistakable without a countdown or false scarcity.
- Constraints: no invented delivery timeline, project price, case study, availability, performance outcome or instant analysis; visible labels, 44px touch targets, honest configuration fallback and mobile-first reading order.
- Open: final scheduling provider and URL, approved investment ranges, call duration, availability, response-time commitment, privacy/POPIA copy and any future qualification threshold.
