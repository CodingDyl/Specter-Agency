# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Motion, and Resend. Deployment target is currently undecided.

## Users

The primary audience is decision-makers at established South African law firms evaluating how to improve their firm's website, search visibility, conversion journey, and enquiry systems.

## Product Purpose

Jurivo is a digital design and growth agency for South African law firms. The marketing site must help qualified firms understand Jurivo's integrated offer and request a no-obligation digital growth audit. A secondary path allows visitors to book a strategy call.

## Positioning

Jurivo combines law-firm positioning, web experience, search visibility, conversion optimisation, tracking, and enquiry automation into one measurable client-acquisition system rather than selling a website as an isolated deliverable.

## Operating Context

Visitors are likely comparing professional-service partners while protecting an established firm's reputation. They need to recognise legal-sector fluency, commercial discipline, and diagnostic competence before sharing their firm's details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Black Label is the selected canonical brand and homepage direction. The two rejected concepts remain only as unlinked internal references until the owner chooses to remove them.
- The primary conversion is a Growth Audit Request; the secondary conversion is a Strategy Call.
- Audit forms collect only practical firm and contact information and must not imply that automated analysis has already occurred.
- Firms may request an audit without an existing website; the website field is optional and must never block the enquiry.
- Successful audit delivery redirects to a dedicated no-index thank-you page that explains the next step and offers a strategy-call path without invented timelines.
- The site must be responsive, accessible, SEO-ready, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Resend is the intended email delivery service. Production sender, recipient, calendar URL, analytics, privacy copy, and deployment target remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Jurivo.
- Market: South Africa; copy uses South African English and `.co.za` examples.
- Voice: precise, commercially intelligent, controlled, and authoritative.
- Do not resemble a generic web-design agency, generic SaaS product, startup, traditional law firm, or television-fandom property.
- No legal clichés such as gavels, scales, wigs, briefcases, staged handshakes, or fake lawyers.
- Black Label is the selected, binding visual direction: cinematic carbon-to-paper rhythm, commanding typography, restrained wine, and deliberate motion without luxury cliché.

## Evidence on Hand

- No verified client logos, testimonials, case studies, performance statistics, awards, or commercial claims are available. The site must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Diagnose before prescribing.
- Treat a law firm's reputation as an asset the digital experience must substantiate.
- Demonstrate an end-to-end growth system, not a menu of disconnected services.
- Earn trust through specificity and restraint, never invented proof.
- Make the audit request clear, credible, and low-friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, labelled forms, sufficient colour contrast, minimum 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, and no information conveyed by colour alone.
